HOW IT WORKS

From analysis to user-authorized execution

1

Market analysis

NathauxFX evaluates market structure, technical conditions and fundamental information for supported markets such as EURUSD and Gold.

2

Signals and risk controls

The platform can surface trading setups, entry context, stop-loss and target information, while applying configured risk rules and trading limits.

3

Connect your own cTrader account

Users can authorize a supported cTrader brokerage account. NathauxFX does not take custody of brokerage funds; the connected account remains with the user's broker.

4

Optional automated execution

If the user enables automation, NathauxFX can send orders to the connected account according to configured platform rules. The user can disable automation.

5

Email signal sharing

Signals and trading information can be shared or delivered by email when the relevant feature is used.

Important: Trading involves risk. Signals and automation do not guarantee profits, win rates or successful trades.
