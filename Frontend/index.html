Trading software — not a broker

NathauxFX is an independent software platform for market analysis, signals, risk management and optional trade automation. NathauxFX does not act as a broker and does not accept trading deposits or hold brokerage funds.

When automation is enabled, orders are sent to the user's own connected cTrader brokerage account under the permissions the user authorizes. Trading decisions, broker selection and account funding remain under the user's control.
