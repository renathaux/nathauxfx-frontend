Trading risk

Forex, gold and other leveraged financial products can move rapidly and may result in substantial losses. You can lose some or all of the capital committed to trading.

No guaranteed performance

NathauxFX provides software tools, market analysis, signals, alerts, risk controls and optional automation. No signal, strategy, automated rule, historical result or platform feature guarantees future profits, a particular win rate, or protection from loss.

Software, not brokerage custody

NathauxFX is not presented as a broker and does not accept trading deposits or hold brokerage funds. When a brokerage integration is used, the trading account remains with the user's chosen broker.

Automated trading

Automated execution can increase both the speed of trading and the speed of losses. Users are responsible for reviewing their settings, connected brokerage account, risk limits and whether automation is enabled.

Third-party services

Brokerage providers, cTrader and other third-party services operate independently from NathauxFX. Their availability, execution, pricing, fees, outages and account rules can affect trading results.

User responsibility

Use only capital you can afford to lose and consider your own financial circumstances, experience and risk tolerance. If you need individualized financial or investment advice, consult an appropriately qualified professional.
