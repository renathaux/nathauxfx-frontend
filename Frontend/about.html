ABOUT NATHAUXFX

Trading analysis and automation software

NathauxFX is an independent software platform designed to help traders analyze EURUSD and Gold, organize trading decisions, apply risk controls, receive alerts, share signals by email, and optionally automate user-authorized trade execution through cTrader.

What NathauxFX is — and what it is not

Software platformMarket structure, technical signals, fundamental insights, risk tools, alerts and automation features.
Not a brokerNathauxFX does not provide brokerage custody and does not accept trading deposits.
Your own broker accountConnected trading accounts remain with the user's chosen brokerage provider.
No guaranteed outcomesTrading involves risk. NathauxFX does not promise profits, returns, win rates or successful trades.

How automation works

When a user enables an automation feature and authorizes a supported cTrader connection, NathauxFX can send orders to that user's connected brokerage account according to the configured platform rules. The user controls whether automation is enabled and remains responsible for the connected account, broker and trading risk.

NathauxFX provides software tools and market information. Nothing on the platform is a guarantee of financial performance.
